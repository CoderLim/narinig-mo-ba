## Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough: before the first customer

Open the game from the creators' itch.io page and let the browser build finish loading. Click into the game so it has pointer focus. If the loading screen repeatedly refuses to progress, a developer has suggested trying a private/incognito browser window.

Before you start moving quickly, learn the four basic interactions: left click for taking or placing items, hold left click for returning an item, right-click interaction for checking prices, and pointer movement toward the edge of the scene for looking around.

The best Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough advice for the opening is simple: **do not rush**. The game is short, so skipping dialogue to save thirty seconds can cost you more context than it saves.

## The repeatable customer routine

Most of the working-simulator flow can be handled with the same checklist.

First, read the customer's full request. Look for the product area only after you know the item and quantity. Second, pick up the requested product. Third, check the price when the transaction requires it. Fourth, move the product to the counter. Fifth, handle the customer's payment and return the correct change. Sixth, let the conversation finish.

Use that sequence every time this Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough says “complete the order.” The routine should become automatic so your attention can move from mechanics to story details.

If you grab the wrong product, return it instead of leaving several items scattered around the interface. A player on the official discussion reported trouble returning some items in an earlier build, and the developers have acknowledged that the original four-day jam project had rough edges. If an object genuinely refuses to reset, restart the immediate sequence only after you are sure it is not still waiting for another interaction.

## If you cannot find the requested product

Move through the store views slowly. The illustrated packaging can make nearby products look similar at a glance, especially when the camera is moving. Read the request again before clicking another item.

A reliable Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough should not turn the shop into a memorization test. The point of the first requests is to teach you how the store is organized. Once you understand the views, later orders become much easier.

Do not assume the product is missing just because it is not in the first shelf view. Check the areas around the counter and other store sections before concluding that the scene is stuck.

## If the customer will not continue

There are four common things to check.

1. Make sure the full requested order is on the counter or has been handed over correctly.
2. Confirm that you have inspected or calculated the price when the scene expects it.
3. Check the payment and change step.
4. Wait for dialogue, animation, or audio to finish before clicking again.

Players have reported occasional progression issues in the official itch.io comments, including a customer not moving after receiving an order. Since the game began as a four-day jam entry, a genuine bug is possible. But in many story games, “stuck” simply means the current interaction has not finished.

This Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough recommends restarting only after you have checked the transaction from beginning to end.

## How to handle the money sequence

The money system is there to make you feel like a storekeeper rather than a dialogue viewer. A customer may hand over more than the price, which means you need to return change.

Do not interpret extra denominations as a bug. One official discussion thread includes a player complaining that a customer paid more than the minimum necessary, which shows that change-making is an intentional part of the experience players encounter.

For each transaction, calculate the total, compare it with the amount given, and return the difference. If you are uncertain, slow down. The Narinig Mo Ba walkthrough goal is consistency, not speed.

## If the camera makes you uncomfortable

Several players in the official comments mention high mouse sensitivity or motion sickness when turning between store views. One player said they were able to finish by moving the mouse more carefully.

Use small movements and pause after reaching the view you want. There is no reward for whipping the camera from side to side. A calm pointer also makes product selection easier.
