## Narinig Mo Ba download for macOS

The macOS build requires more explanation. Players have reported that the application may refuse to open normally. The developers responded that the Mac versions are unsigned and unlicensed because their team does not currently have Apple devices or the Apple developer licenses needed to officially sign the build.

In another official reply, the developer suggested opening **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and using **Open Anyway** for the downloaded app. This is a standard macOS path for manually allowing an app that Apple has blocked because it is not notarized, but you should only bypass the warning when you trust the source.

That is why the source of your Narinig Mo Ba download matters. If the app came from an unknown mirror, do not use “Open Anyway” merely because a guide tells you to. If you do not want to bypass the Mac warning at all, use the official browser version instead.
