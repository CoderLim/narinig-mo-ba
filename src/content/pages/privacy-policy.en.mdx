## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **narinig mo ba** (narinigmoba.site) handles information when you visit the site, play the embedded browser game, or create an account.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information** — If you sign up: name, email, and credentials needed for authentication.
2. **Usage Data** — Pages visited, approximate play sessions, and basic analytics events (for example GA4 or Plausible when configured).
3. **Device Information** — Browser type and general device class used to keep the site compatible.
4. **Cookies / Local Storage** — Session cookies and preference storage (theme, locale).

We do **not** receive your gameplay progress from the third-party game iframe unless that embed sets its own cookies under its own domain.

## Embedded Game

The playable build is loaded from a third-party host (`games.pokepath.site`). That host may process its own technical logs. The original game is published by its creators on [itch.io](https://doppie.itch.io/narinig-mo-ba). This site is an independent fan/play mirror for convenience and is not the official publisher.

## How We Use Information

- Operate and improve the website.
- Provide optional accounts, support, and admin features.
- Measure aggregate traffic and fix bugs.
- Respond to abuse or legal requests when required.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited sharing may occur with infrastructure providers (hosting, email, analytics) under their terms, or when required by law.
