## Why the current game is difficult on touchscreens

To understand the Narinig Mo Ba mobile delay, look at how the game is controlled. You pick up and place items, return items, inspect prices, look around the store, and advance dialogue with different mouse interactions. That is why a true Narinig Mo Ba mobile update is more useful than a simple browser wrapper. The game’s shop routine is easy to understand, but its interactions were designed for a pointer.
